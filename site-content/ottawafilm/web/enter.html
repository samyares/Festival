Rules & terms

01 Films completed on or after Jan 1, 2020 are eligible for consideration.
02 All entries must be either in English or subtitled in English. Dialogue lists are not accepted.
03 Filmmakers may submit more than one film, provided each film is submitted separately.
04 Short films must be 50 minutes or less.
05 Feature films must be 50 minutes or more.
06 We accept films of all genres.
