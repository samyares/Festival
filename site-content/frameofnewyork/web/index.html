Terms & Rules

  1. Eligibility — The festival is open to filmmakers worldwide. Submissions must be the original work of the entrant and must not infringe on third-party rights.
  2. Submission Format — Films must be submitted in a digital format. Ensure compatibility with common streaming platforms.
  3. Language — Non-English films must include English subtitles.
  4. Duration — There is no restriction on film length; however, films may be categorized based on duration (short, feature-length, etc.).
  5. Original Content — All submissions must be original works. Plagiarism or unauthorized use of copyrighted material will result in disqualification.
  6. Promotional Use — Entrants allow the festival to use excerpts, trailers, and stills for promotional purposes.
  7. Selection Process — Films will be evaluated by a jury panel. The festival reserves the right to determine the final selection and award winners.
