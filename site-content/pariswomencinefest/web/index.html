How It Works

From submission to celebration in three simple steps.

1. Submit Your Film

Enter your film or screenplay through FilmFreeway in any of our 26 categories, open to independent women filmmakers worldwide.

2. Jury Review

Our judging panel evaluates every entry on originality, storytelling, direction, production quality, and overall impact.

3. Win & Celebrate

Winners receive digital laurels and official PDF certificates, and their work is celebrated on our winners page and social channels.
