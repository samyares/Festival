( About )

The Indiana Independent Film Festival invites filmmakers from across the globe to join us in celebrating the art of film through our online platform. We are proud to offer a space that showcases a diverse range of genres, providing filmmakers the opportunity to present their unique visions to an enthusiastic and wide-reaching audience.

Our festival is dedicated to recognizing and honoring the creativity and talent of filmmakers of all ages and backgrounds. Each season, our esteemed panel of judges will review the top submissions online and select winners in various categories.

Winning films will be awarded official certificates and receive extensive exposure through announcements on our social media platforms and website. Join us in this exciting event to connect with other filmmakers, gain recognition, and celebrate the power of storytelling through films.

( Rules & Terms )

About screening: selected winner films will be screened online on our official platform as part of the festival. If any winner does not wish to participate in the screening, they must notify us before the event day.

  1. Submission Format — All films must be submitted via FilmFreeway as a secure online screener (YouTube, Vimeo, or FilmFreeway upload). No physical media will be accepted.
  2. Language — Films in any language are welcome, but non-English films must include English subtitles.
  3. Completion Date — There is no strict production year restriction, but we encourage submitting recent work (preferably completed within the past 3 years).
  4. Premiere Status — We do not require premiere status. Films that have screened at other festivals or online are welcome.
  5. Genres — All genres and styles are accepted: narrative, documentary, animation, experimental, and more.
  6. Multiple Submissions — Filmmakers may submit multiple entries, but each film must be submitted separately with its own entry fee.
  7. Selection Process — All films are reviewed by our programming team and jury. Selected filmmakers will be notified by the listed notification date.
  8. Online Screening for Winners — Winning films will be screened during the festival on our online platform and promoted via our website and social media.
